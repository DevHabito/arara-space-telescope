# Literature, provenance, and originality boundaries

## Established outside ARARA

Threshold calibration, matched filtering, linear-algebra projections, holdout studies, multiple-testing correction, and injection-recovery tests are existing techniques. Their use inside the ARARA simulator does not make these ideas original discoveries.

## Project-specific research constructions

The historical synthetic generator, causal alignment anchor, projected-reference score, frozen template-search dimensions, simulation regimes, and milestone acceptance gates are ARARA-specific configurations under investigation. The archived evidence supports findings conditional on the generator, not an astronomical detector claim.

## Not established

There is no demonstrated real-sky recall, real-sky FPR, astronomical PPV, stable physical onset estimate, flight-qualified instrument, closed photometric noise budget, or completed M38B operational evaluation.

## External references (verify any later claim against original texts)

4. Lightkurve. Searching for Kepler, K2, and TESS data products. https://lightkurve.github.io/lightkurve/tutorials/1-getting-started/searching-for-data-products.html . Data-access instructions, not evidence of detector performance.
5. Lean project. https://github.com/leanprover/lean4 and https://github.com/leanprover/lean-action . Machine verification of stated theorems only.

## Research claim checklist

For each future finding, record source and license of data, model assumptions, seed plan, exact configuration, code revision, immutable preregistration, comparison baseline, raw counts, interval definition, frozen tests, outcome state, and known failure conditions. Separate software test, mathematical theorem, simulation result, and astronomical measurement.
